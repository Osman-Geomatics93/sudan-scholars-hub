# Platform Knowledge

## About Sudan Scholars Hub
- A bilingual (English/Arabic) platform helping Sudanese students find scholarships and study resources
- Website: https://sudanscholars.com
- Features: Scholarship search, Study materials, Flashcards with spaced repetition, Quiz battles, AI assistant, Leaderboard, CV builder, IELTS/TOEFL practice

## Popular Scholarship Programs
- Turkiye Burslari (Turkey Scholarships) — fully funded, all levels
- Chevening Scholarships (UK) — fully funded Master's
- DAAD (Germany) — various funding levels
- Fulbright (USA) — fully funded graduate studies
- Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) — fully funded, all levels

## Telegram Community
- Students use Telegram groups for collaboration and support
